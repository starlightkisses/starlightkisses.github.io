How to bake ✿

Build a cake for Xavier's birthday and share!

1

Pick a station

The round icons on the shelf under the cake are your stations: Plate, Cake, Frosting, Trim, Message, the six Deco spots and Candles. Swipe the row sideways to see them all, then tap one to open it.

2

Choose your cake toppings

Tap any picture in the panel to add it to your cake. Tap a different one to swap it. Tap None to take it off. You'll see the cake change right away.

The cake has six Deco spots, so you can put a different topping on each one. They are named by where they sit on the cake:

  • Back left / Back right: the two spots at the back, farthest from you
  • Middle left / Middle right: the two spots in the middle
  • Front left / Front right: the two spots at the front, closest to you

Each spot can hold strawberries, blueberries, gold stars, sugar pearls, hearts, sparkles, flowers or cherries. The two back spots can also hold wafers and wafer bunnies.

3

Change the colors

Some stations have little color dots. Tap a dot to recolor that part. On the Cake station you get two rows: the top row colors the cake itself, and the bottom row colors the cream lines between the layers.

4

Add candles

Open the Candles station to pick how many you want, whether they sit in an arch or a circle, and what color they are.

5

Kitchen buttons

↶ Undo takes back your last change. 🎲 Chef's pick gives you a random cake. Fresh batch wipes the cake so you can start over. My other works opens my other creations.

✨ Tip: Not sure where to start? Hit Chef's pick, then change the parts you don't like. When your cake is done, take a screenshot to save it!

✦ How to Play ✦

The Xavier Bingo is a moonlit bingo game — mark off phrases on your card as they're called, and race to complete a line (or the whole card).

Playing a round

  1. Press Begin to get your card and the first call.
  2. Each call shows a number and a phrase, e.g. #7 — a clue.
  3. Find the matching cell on your card and tap it. A wrong tap just shakes — try again.
  4. Once you mark the right cell, the next call appears automatically.

Winning

  • Bingo — complete any full row, column, or diagonal. You can then choose to Claim Bingo and finish, or Keep Going toward a Full House.
  • Full House — mark every cell on the card. This is the top tier and gets its own certificate.

The Skip button

Stuck on the current call? Tap Skip to give up on it and move straight to the next one. The skipped cell stays unmarked for the rest of that round — it won't be called again — so use it if a phrase has you stumped, not just to speed things up.

After you finish

You'll get a shareable certificate — download it as an image, and if you got a Bingo or Full House, you can submit your time to the leaderboard. Use New Card or Play Again to start fresh anytime.

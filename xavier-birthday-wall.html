✮⋆˙ Welcome to the Wall of Stars ❀★

A birthday gift for Xavier, wished up by Little Stars.

This is a little corner of the sky where everyone can leave him a wish. Every note you pin stays on the wall, so it keeps growing bigger

.✦ ݁˖ To leave a wish 💌

Tap on the "Leave a message for Xavier" button at the bottom. You can opt to write a message or doodle or both.

  • Write a message of up to 180 characters, or
  • Doodle it by drawing with your finger. You can change the color and thickness, and undo or clear.
  • Then pick your note's size, tilt and color. A preview shows how it'll look.

Add your name and tap Pin it to the wall and it will fly into place automatically ♡

📝 Note: incase of a mistake-

  • A small pencil icon appears on the notes you wrote. Tap it to edit or delete your wish.
  • The pencil only shows on the same device and browser you used to write the note. So if you open the wall on a different device or through a link on another browser, the pencil won't appear and you won't be able to change your note.
  • Doodles can't be edited, but you can delete one and draw a new one.

.✦ ݁˖ Explore the wall

  • Read a wish: tap on it. It will get bigger.
  • Send love: if you really like someone's wish tap the ♡ to send it a heart.
    Disclaimer: the number of hearts has nothing to do with where a wish sits on the wall. Every wish is placed on the wall automatically.
  • Find yours: to find your wish admist all, click on Find and search with the name you gave inside your wish
  • Share: to share the link to a friend click on Share button
  • Snapshot: and if you want a whole screenshot of all the love and wishes our prince is getting, click on Snapshot to a picture of the whole wall.

.✦ ݁˖ Night sky 🌌

Tap on ✦ Constellation to turn the wall into a starry sky. Every wish you see on the wall, becomes a golden star, joining into a constellation around our Xavier. If you tap a star you can read its wish.

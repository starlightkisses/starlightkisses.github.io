How to Play ✦

Xavier Memory Match is a cozy card-matching game — find every matching pair before you run out of moves (or time, in Challenge mode)!

How to play: Tap any two cards to flip them. If they match, they stay face-up. If not, they flip back — remember their spots for next time!

Difficulty levels:

  • Easy — 12 pairs, no time pressure
  • Medium — 16 pairs, a bit more of a challenge
  • Hard — 20 pairs, for sharp memories
  • Challenge — 24 pairs, beat the 4:00 clock!

Match every pair to win. Good luck! ⋆｡✦
